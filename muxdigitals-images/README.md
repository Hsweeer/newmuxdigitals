# MuxDigitals LinkedIn (Company-Page Safe)

## Why it looked broken before
LinkedIn places your **profile logo over the bottom-left of the banner**.
Putting logo + MuxDigitals on the left made LinkedIn cover it → double logo + unreadable text.

## Upload these

### Profile logo (includes MuxDigitals + small tagline)
- `linkedin/USE-THIS-profile-logo-800.png`
- Also: `linkedin/profile-logo-300.png` (LinkedIn native size)

### Banner — content starts AFTER the logo safe zone (left ~430px clear)
- Company page (official): `linkedin/USE-THIS-company-banner-1128x191.png`
- Wide cover: `linkedin/USE-THIS-banner-1584x396.png`
